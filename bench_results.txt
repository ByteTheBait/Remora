Notes:
  * Remora runs the real Mamba selective-scan kernel over sharded
    weights, streaming one layer at a time from disk via mmap. Its
    TPS is measured from the kernel's own 'gen tok/s' line.
  * remora_llama is the libllama-based driver: it produces real Mamba
    outputs from sharded weights via the same llama.cpp numerics and
    sampler as llama-cli. It exists to compare RSS / TTFT / TPS between
    sharded-weight streaming and monolithic llama-cli.
  * 'TTFT' for Remora is the kernel's own ttft (time to first generated
    token); remora_llama's is wall time until 'indexed N tensors' is
    printed (model-graph ready); llama.cpp's is (total_ms - eval_ms),
    which still includes prompt processing.
  * RSS is sampled from `ps -o rss=` at 20 Hz; peak is the max.
